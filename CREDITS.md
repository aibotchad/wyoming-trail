into `assets/img/sprites.png`.

## Backdrop and plant photos

Hunting and foraging backdrops and plant photos (`field_*.jpg`, `plant_*.jpg`)
are from Wikimedia Commons; authors, licences and source links are in
`assets/data/credits.json` and on the in-game credits screen. Plant photos show
the species, not necessarily plants photographed in Wyoming. Weather odds,
hitchhiker outcomes and GPS mishaps are invented for the game.

## Map

`assets/img/map.jpg` is stitched from zoom level 8 tiles of the USGS National Map
(`basemap.nationalmap.gov`, USGSTopo layer; public domain), cropped to longitude
-111.35 to -103.75, latitude 40.75 to 45.25 (Web Mercator) and tinted.

Road lines and mileages (`assets/data/road_paths.json`, `map.json`) come from the
OSRM demo router, using map data (c) OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL).

## Text

The "TRUE STORY" facts are summarised from English Wikipedia articles on each
place (CC BY-SA 4.0). "Local legends", events, prices, services, road risk and
scenery ratings are invented for the game.
